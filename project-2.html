Commercial Vision & Strategic Value

The Commercial Complex & Retail Hub is an iconic commercial asset created by Shreesthi Infracon Pvt. Ltd., positioned directly along the high-traffic Shaheed Path arterial highway in Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow.

Conceived as a modern destination for high-end retail, financial institutions, specialized diagnostic centers, and corporate office suites, this development enjoys exceptional visibility with an expansive highway facade. The building incorporates high-capacity floor plates, dual high-speed elevators, dedicated lower basement parking, 100% DG power backup provisions, and state-of-the-art seismic-resistant structural engineering.

Situated within 5 minutes of Ekana International Cricket Stadium, Phoenix Palassio Luxury Mall, and Medanta Super Speciality Hospital, the complex sits at the epicenter of Lucknow’s elite commercial zone, ensuring sustained capital appreciation and premium rental yields for commercial investors.
