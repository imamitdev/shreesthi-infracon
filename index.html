About Us

Shreesthi Infracon

Pvt. Ltd. • Registered Real Estate Developer
“Creating the Canvas for a New Tomorrow.”

Established in 2019, Shresthi Infracon Pvt. Ltd. has been building a trusted presence in the real estate and construction industry, turning aspirations into thoughtfully planned spaces and meaningful investments. With a commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction, we have successfully completed three projects, earning the trust of our customers along the way.

At Shresthi Infracon, we understand that a property is more than just a piece of land—it is a dream, an investment, and a foundation for the future. Our mission is to make every investment experience seamless, reliable, and rewarding.

Our comprehensive real estate services include residential and commercial property development, plotted developments, property investment solutions, construction services, project planning, and real estate consultancy. From identifying the right opportunity to delivering quality spaces, we strive to be with our customers at every step.

Driven by integrity, innovation, and a vision for sustainable growth, Shresthi Infracon continues to create spaces that inspire confidence and investments that build a better tomorrow.
