Project Overview & Architectural Story

Greenfield Plotted Township represents Shreesthi Infracon’s flagship plotted development, conceived to address the accelerating demand for high-caliber residential land along Lucknow's fastest-appreciating investment corridor—the Kisan Path (Outer Ring Road) and Sultanpur Highway interchange.

Spanning a unified 25-acre contiguous land parcel, the township was meticulously planned by civil engineers and urban designers to provide an idyllic retreat from metropolitan congestion without sacrificing accessibility. The master layout establishes a clear arterial hierarchy: a monumental 40-foot black-top main entrance avenue seamlessly distributes traffic into serene 30-foot residential cross-lanes, guaranteeing zero bottle-necks and unmatched peace for residents.

Unlike unorganized land layouts, Greenfield Plotted Township incorporates complete underground stormwater conduits, individual plot boundary demarcation pillars, dedicated avenue plantations, solar street illumination, and a fully monitored gated perimeter. Over 180+ discerning families and land investors have already finalized registry and received spot possession.

Key Infrastructure & Engineering Highlights

Wide Blacktop Roads

40-foot main entrance boulevard and 30-foot internal residential lanes with heavy-duty tar paving.

Underground Drainage

Concealed RCC stormwater drains engineered to prevent water-logging during peak monsoons.

Avenue Plantation

Mature evergreen roadside trees and landscaped central community park providing clean air.

Smart Solar Lighting

High-lumen LED solar street light fixtures installed across every intersection for nighttime safety.

Gated Perimeter

Full 7-foot masonry boundary wall with grand entrance security arch and dedicated guard room.

Pillar Demarcation

Each plot is precision-measured by our civil surveyor and pegged with individual concrete corner markers.
