Peaceful Sanctuary • City Proximity

Nature Grove Residential Enclave is an exclusive 12-acre eco-luxury plotted community thoughtfully designed for families aspiring to build custom independent villas surrounded by lush green landscapes, pure air, and quiet tranquility.

Located along the rapidly developing Gomti Nagar Extension periphery, Nature Grove bridges the gap between rural freshness and urban infrastructure. The enclave features 35-foot main entrance avenues and 25-foot inter-sector residential roads, an open landscaped central park with children’s play zones, intelligent solar street lighting, underground water connection provisions, and perimeter boundary demarcation.

With clear revenue Khasra titles, spot registry, and guaranteed Section 143 (Dakhil Kharij) mutation, buyers can initiate villa construction immediately or hold their plot as a high-growth wealth appreciation asset.
