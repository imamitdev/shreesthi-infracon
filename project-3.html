Description

Serene Nature-First Residential Enclave • Urban Convenience

Nature Grove Residential Enclave was envisioned and developed by Shreesthi Infracon to cater to homeowners looking for clean air, natural lush greenery, and a tranquil neighborhood, while remaining within minutes of Gomti Nagar Extension and Shaheed Path.

The community features tree-lined internal streets, dedicated pedestrian jogging paths, landscaped green open pockets, and rainwater recharge pits. All plots have been demarcated with boundary stones and are ready for immediate private villa construction.

Enjoying rapid capital appreciation due to major ongoing infrastructural expansions in the Gomti Nagar Extension belt, Nature Grove is fully delivered with active residents and verified freehold documentation.
